Leo and the Crystal of Worlds — Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Leo and the Crystal of Worlds is a platform game for children and families, made by RMNApps. This policy explains what happens to data when you or your child play the game from Google Play.

Nothing leaves the device

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. It does not collect, send or share any personal information, and it asks for no special permissions. Game progress, collected stars and settings such as the language and the sound are stored only on the device.

Children

The game is made for children aged 6 and up, and for older players too. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links that lead out of the game.

Keeping and deleting data

Game progress stays on the device until you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. We keep no data about you or your child, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes

If a later version adds anything that uses data, for example ads, this page is updated before that version is released, and so is its effective date.
