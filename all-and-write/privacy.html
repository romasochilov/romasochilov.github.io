All & Write — Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

All & Write is a companion app for print-and-play roll & write games, made by RMNApps. This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app from Google Play.

Your games stay on your device

The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your games, scores, cities and settings are stored only on your device, and the app does not send them anywhere. A sheet or a rules PDF leaves the device only when you save or share it yourself.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: one banner on the home screen, and a full-screen ad when a new game starts, at most once every 30 minutes. No ads are shown during play.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and similar app and device identifiers;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with the ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped, and diagnostic data about how the ads perform.

The data is sent encrypted. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Where these choices apply, you can change them at any time with Privacy options at the bottom of the app's home screen.
  • On any Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in the device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Your games stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We keep no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Data that Google collects for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page is updated, and so is its effective date.
