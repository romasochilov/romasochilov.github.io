Leo and the Crystal of Worlds — Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Leo and the Crystal of Worlds is a platform game for children and families, made by RMNApps. This policy explains what happens to data when you or your child play the game from Google Play.

Nothing is sent to us

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. It does not collect, send or share any personal information, and it asks for no special permissions. The game itself never connects to the internet, and it keeps its progress and settings in its own storage on the device, which Android keeps private from other apps. Game progress, collected stars and settings such as the language and the sound are stored on the device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may also keep a copy in your own Google account backup, which we cannot see.

Children

The game is made for children aged 6 and up, and for older players too. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links that lead out of the game.

Keeping and deleting data

Game progress stays on the device until you clear the game's data or uninstall the game; if Android backup is on, Android may restore it when the game is installed again. A copy in your Google account backup, if there is one, is kept and deleted under your Google account's backup settings. The game gives us no data about you or your child, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page is updated and so is its effective date. If a later version adds anything that uses data, for example ads, this page is updated before that version is released, and that version's release notes on Google Play say so.

Contact

Questions about this policy: romasochilov.tr+public@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your address only to reply; if a child writes to us, we reply to that message only and then delete it.
